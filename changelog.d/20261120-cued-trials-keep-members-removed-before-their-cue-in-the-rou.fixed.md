Fixed `Trial.cue` with `sync_group_type` for a member removed from their group during a round before their own cue reached it, for example by a barrier's `late_after` while the rest of the group was already playing. Their trial is now in that round, with their role and their removal reason as `sync_group_outcome`, so `group_round.trials` and exports list them, as for a member removed just after their trial started. Such a trial needs no `sync_group_optional = True`. It runs the grouped `show_trial`, whose barriers let them through without waiting. Choose a solo version in `show_trial` with `self.sync_group is None`, never with `participant.in_sync_group(...)`: `show_trial` is called again during the trial, so its pages must not change when a member leaves.
