Added `participant.latest_sync_group(group_type)`, which returns the participant's current or most recent group of that type even after it has ended or they have left it, so shared state kept in `group.var` can be read after the game, and `participant.sync_group_seat(group_type)`, which returns their 0-based seat in that group in joining order, stable when other members leave, for taking turns or assigning roles. `group.member_in_seat(seat)` returns the current member in a seat, such as the one whose turn it is.
