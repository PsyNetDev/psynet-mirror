Added three live game demos: `demos/experiments/bargaining_live`, in which a cohort of four is paired at random each round to bargain over a stake by proposing and accepting splits, a player who doesn't reach the game leaves the cohort so the next pairing doesn't wait for them, and a game whose player never opens it ends at once with feedback that says the partner didn't arrive; `demos/experiments/public_goods_live`, a repeated public goods game in teams of three who see each other's contributions as they change, with an export of each player's contribution and earnings per round; and `demos/experiments/microsociety_live`, a fishing society that keeps playing one continuous game while members join and leave.
