Pages that come straight after another page in the timeline, such as a run of instruction pages, now have a Back button, so participants can go back and answer the earlier page again. The earlier page appears at once, without waiting for the server. The button sits at the left of the page's buttons, so Next doesn't move. Trial pages and pages after code blocks or conditions have none, and `show_back_button=False` hides it on a page. See "Going back to the previous page" in the timeline documentation.
