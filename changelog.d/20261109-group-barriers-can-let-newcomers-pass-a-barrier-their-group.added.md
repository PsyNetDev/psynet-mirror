Added `GroupBarrier(pass_newcomers=True)` for groups formed with `join_existing_groups=True`: anyone who reaches the barrier after their group has passed it goes straight through, instead of waiting for members who are further on and, with `late_after`, getting those members removed as late. Use it on barriers the group passes once, such as one before the game's rounds. PsyNet now warns at experiment load about a barrier after such a grouper with neither `pass_newcomers=True` nor `stop_top_ups=True`, and about `pass_newcomers=True` on a barrier in a loop. A synchronized trial maker's own initialization barrier now lets newcomers through too.
