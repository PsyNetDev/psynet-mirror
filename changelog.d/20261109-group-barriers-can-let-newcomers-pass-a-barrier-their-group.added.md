Added `GroupBarrier(pass_newcomers=True)` for groups formed with `join_existing_groups=True`: anyone who reaches the barrier after their group has passed it goes straight through, instead of waiting for members who are further on and, with `late_after`, getting those members removed as late. Use it on barriers the group passes once per module, such as one before the game's rounds. Only a release that is the group's pass counts, not a release of only members catching up or one while a member who hasn't left the module is in a later cued round; passing through doesn't run `on_release`. PsyNet now warns at experiment load about a barrier after such a grouper with neither `pass_newcomers=True` nor `stop_top_ups=True`, and about `pass_newcomers=True` on a barrier in a loop.
