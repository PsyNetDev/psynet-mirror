Added `GroupBarrier(pass_newcomers=True)` for groups formed with `join_existing_groups=True`: anyone who reaches the barrier after their group has passed it goes straight through, instead of waiting for members who are further on and, with `late_after`, getting those members removed as late. Use it on barriers the group passes once per module, such as one before the game's rounds. Every release of the group counts as its pass, but not a member released on a time-out; passing through doesn't run `on_release`. PsyNet now warns at experiment load about a barrier after such a grouper with neither `pass_newcomers=True` nor `stop_top_ups=True`, and about `pass_newcomers=True` on a barrier in a loop.
