Added `Trial.sync_group_outcome`, which records how a group's round ended for each player of a trial made by `Trial.cue()`: `"released"` once a `GroupBarrier` releases the group, or why the player left the group, such as `"late"`, `"left_experiment"` or `"group_dissolved"` (the values are listed in `psynet.sync.SyncGroupOutcome`). Feedback pages and exports can read it instead of working it out from current group membership. The `ultimatum_pairing` and `lowest_unique_number` demos use it, and `lowest_unique_number` stores each room's result in `node.var.result`.
