Added `Trial.sync_group_outcome`, which records how a group's round ended for each player of a trial in a group's round, made by `Trial.cue()` or a trial maker with `sync_group_type`: `"stayed"` once a `GroupBarrier` in the trial's pages (such as in `show_trial`) releases the group with the player in it, after which it doesn't change, or, before that, why the player left the group, such as `"late"`, `"left_experiment"` or `"group_dissolved"` (the values are listed in `psynet.sync.SyncGroupOutcome`). Leaving the group after the release or after the trial's pages, for example by being late at a later barrier, shows in `participant.sync_group_removal_reason(group_type)` instead. In a trial maker's round that no barrier in the trial released, the trials that members still in the group completed record `"stayed"` when the group's next trials are prepared. Feedback pages and exports can read it instead of working it out from current group membership. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo uses it. A member failed by a barrier's `late_action="fail"` or a group's `below_quorum="fail"` now leaves the group before being failed, so `ParticipantFailRoutine`s no longer see it in `participant.active_sync_groups`.
