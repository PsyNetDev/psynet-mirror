Added `Trial.sync_group_outcome`, which records how a group's round ended for each player of a trial made by `Trial.cue()`: `"stayed"` once a `GroupBarrier` in the trial's pages (such as in `show_trial`) releases the group, or why the player left the group during those pages, such as `"late"`, `"left_experiment"` or `"group_dissolved"` (the values are listed in `psynet.sync.SyncGroupOutcome`). Once the trial's pages have ended, the outcome no longer changes. Feedback pages and exports can read it instead of working it out from current group membership. The `ultimatum_pairing` and `lowest_unique_number` demos use it, and `lowest_unique_number` stores each room's result in `node.var.result`. A member failed by a barrier's `late_action="fail"` or a group's `below_quorum="fail"` now leaves the group before being failed, so `ParticipantFailRoutine`s no longer see it in `participant.active_sync_groups`.
