Added asynchronous LocalStorage video answer uploads for in-place experiments: accepted-answer reservations, bounded recovery of lost acceptance responses without duplicate advancement or extended deadlines, a bounded browser queue, size-based upload allowances, authenticated receipt with exclusive upload ownership and crash cleanup, bounded video decoding and deposit, and deadline handling that fails the affected trial while preserving earlier answers. Missing, oversized, and capacity-limited recordings, including unavailable browser transport, preserve answer submission and fail only the affected trial at its deadline. Missing-recording failures release feedback and performance-check waits without bypassing configured performance checks or running late analysis; ordinary analysis-failure feedback is preserved. Chain selection prevents assigning the same failed recording answer to its participant again. Legacy full-reload experiments, other storage backends, and bots retain the existing answer-upload path. Full document navigation can abandon queued uploads without holding navigation.

Added timeout callables to `wait_while` and `while_loop`, evaluated once when waiting starts. Dependent recording waits account for upload and processing deadlines. Resolved waits exit without timing out when participants return to inactive tabs.

Added `wait_for_recording` for bounded non-trial playback waits that preserve participant navigation when media is unavailable; experiment authors choose a fallback by checking whether the recording was deposited.
