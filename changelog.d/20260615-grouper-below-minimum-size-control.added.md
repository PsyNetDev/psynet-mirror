Added `SimpleGrouper(below_quorum=...)`, which decides what happens to the remaining members of a group without top-ups when it drops below its `quorum` and is dissolved: `"fail"` fails them, so one member who quits or is late ends the session of everyone who was on time, and `"continue_without_group"` lets them continue the experiment without the group, and their next page tells them why ("Your partner is no longer in your group. Please continue."). It must be given when groups are designed to shrink: when `quorum` is below `initial_group_size` (checked by the grouper), or when a `GroupBarrier` with `late_after` acts on the group type, directly or through `also_leave` (checked when the experiment loads). Otherwise it defaults to `"fail"`. The experiment also refuses to load when a barrier with `late_action="leave_group"` acts on a group whose grouper has `below_quorum="fail"`, which would let the late member carry on while the members who were on time are failed; for barriers the load-time check can't see, such as those built in `show_trial`, PsyNet logs a warning when that happens. A group formed with `join_existing_groups=True` is not dissolved while it accepts newcomers; "Groups below quorum" in the synchronization docs gives the full rule. In the 14.0 release candidates this was `fail_participants_below_min_size=False`, which now raises with a pointer; use `below_quorum="continue_without_group"` instead.
