Added `SimpleGrouper(below_quorum=...)`, which decides what happens to the remaining members of a group without top-ups when it drops below its `quorum` and is dissolved: `"fail"` (default) fails them, so one member who quits or is late ends the session of everyone who was on time, and `"continue_without_group"` lets them continue the experiment without the group, and their next page tells them why ("Your partner is no longer in your group. Please continue."). Pass `below_quorum="continue_without_group"` to let a pair or group carry on short-handed. A group formed with `join_existing_groups=True` is not dissolved while it accepts newcomers; "Groups below quorum" in the synchronization docs gives the full rule. When a `GroupBarrier` with `late_action="leave_group"` dissolves such a group and fails the members who were on time, PsyNet logs a warning. In the 14.0 release candidates this was `fail_participants_below_min_size=False`, which now raises with a pointer; use `below_quorum="continue_without_group"` instead.
