Added `SimpleGrouper(below_quorum=...)`, which decides what happens to the remaining members of a group without top-ups when it drops below its `quorum` and is dissolved: `"fail"` (default) fails them, `"continue_without_group"` lets them continue the experiment without the group, and their next page tells them why ("Your partner is no longer in your group. Please continue."), and a module-level function of `participant` and `group` decides member by member (if it raises or returns anything else, the error is logged and that member is failed). A group formed with `join_existing_groups=True` waits for newcomers instead of dissolving until `group.stop_top_ups()` is called; `below_quorum` applies from then on. When a `GroupBarrier` with `late_action="leave_group"` would dissolve such a group and fail the members who were on time, PsyNet warns when the experiment loads (for barriers in the timeline) and logs a warning when it happens. In the 14.0 release candidates this was `fail_participants_below_min_size=False`, which now raises with a pointer; use `below_quorum="continue_without_group"` instead.
