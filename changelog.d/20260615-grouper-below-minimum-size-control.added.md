Added `SimpleGrouper(below_quorum=...)`, which decides what happens to the remaining members of a group without top-ups when it drops below its `quorum` and is dissolved: `"fail"` (default) fails them, `"leave_group"` lets them continue the experiment without the group, and their next page tells them why ("Your partner is no longer in your group. Please continue."), and a module-level function of `participant` and `group` decides member by member (if it raises or returns anything else, the error is logged and that member is failed). Passing anything but `"fail"` with `join_existing_groups=True` raises an error, because a group that accepts top-ups waits for newcomers instead of dissolving. PsyNet logs a warning when `late_action="leave_group"` dissolves a group whose `below_quorum` fails the members who were on time. In the 14.0 release candidates this was `fail_participants_below_quorum=False`; use `below_quorum="leave_group"` instead.
