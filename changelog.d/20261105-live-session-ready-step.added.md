A live session starts once every player is ready: a game page says so by itself after it measures its clock against the server's, and bots are ready at once. Until then the game rejects messages, and a player who leaves isn't ready any more. At the game's `ready_timeout`, 30 seconds unless set, it starts without the players who aren't ready. A game sets its deadlines in `on_start`, and a page disables its controls until then with `psynet.liveSession.disableUntilStarted`, checks `started`, or waits for `onStarted`. The state and views are dicts, with PsyNet's `live_session` entry, and `Ready.saved_messages(session_id)` says when each player became ready.
