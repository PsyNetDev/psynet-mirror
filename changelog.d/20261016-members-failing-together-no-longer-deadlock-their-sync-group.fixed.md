Fixed a database deadlock when two members of a sync group failed at the same moment, for example when both timed out waiting for a third member. When a group without top-ups drops below its minimum size, its remaining members are still failed (or, with `fail_participants_below_min_size=False`, removed) straight away, except that a member who is in the middle of a request at that moment is now handled by the background barrier check within about a second, once that request finishes, instead of being waited for. A new group leader is likewise chosen only from members who are not busy.
