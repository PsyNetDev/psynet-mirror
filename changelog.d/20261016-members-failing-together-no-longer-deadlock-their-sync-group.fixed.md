Fixed a database deadlock when two members of a sync group failed at the same moment, for example when both timed out waiting for a third member. When a group without top-ups drops below its `quorum`, its remaining members are still all failed (or, with `below_quorum="continue_without_group"`, removed) together, straight away, except that if a member is in the middle of a request at that moment, the background barrier check dissolves the group within about a second, once that request finishes, instead of waiting for it. A new group leader is likewise chosen only from members who are not busy.
