Added `remove_participant(participant, reason="finished")` for members who leave a group after playing their part, such as players leaving a cohort after their last round. Their leaving never dissolves the group, so the last players are no longer told that too few members remained, and a `GroupPrompt` shows them as "(finished)". A group below its quorum is still handled at its next `GroupBarrier` as usual: it waits for a newcomer while it accepts top-ups and is dissolved otherwise (see "Groups below quorum" in the synchronization docs).
