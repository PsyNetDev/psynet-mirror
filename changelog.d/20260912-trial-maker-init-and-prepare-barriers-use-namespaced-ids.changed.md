Trial-maker ``GroupBarrier`` ids for participant init and trial prepare are namespaced with the trial-maker id (``{id}__init_participant``, ``{id}__prepare_trial``), so two trial makers in one timeline no longer share a waiting pool. ``SimpleGrouper`` without an explicit ``id_`` now defaults to ``{group_type}_grouper_{initial_group_size}`` rather than ``{group_type}_grouper``; pass ``id_`` to keep a shared waiting pool across sizes.
