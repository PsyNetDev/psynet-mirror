`psynet.websocket` connects a timeline page to the experiment over Dallinger's `/experiment-socket` route. `send(type, message)` sends a typed message, which the experiment's `handle_websocket_message` receives on the `psynet` channel. `handle(type, callback)` receives the experiment's messages of that type, and `onConnect(callback)` runs on every connect. Only the participant's current page can connect: once the participant moves on, the earlier page's connections close, including in other tabs, and don't reconnect.
