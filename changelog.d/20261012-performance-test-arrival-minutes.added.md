Added `--arrival-minutes` to `psynet performance-test local`, `ssh` and `psynet audit performance-test`, which spreads each test's bots evenly over that many minutes, as participants arrive while a study is open. With `--n-bots auto` it applies only to the confirmation test, so the search's tests stay short, and must be shorter than an hour. It can't be combined with `--stagger`.
