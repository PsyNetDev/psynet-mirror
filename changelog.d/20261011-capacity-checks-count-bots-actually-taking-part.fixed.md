Performance tests now count as exceeding their limits when fewer than 90% of the requested bots were ever taking part at the same time, and when either ``/timeline`` or ``/response`` alone has a 95th-percentile response time over ``--max-p95-ms``. Before, a capacity search could report a capacity far above the load it actually applied, because one process can only start about 8 bots a second. A test is no longer failed by a single stray error: it may have up to 0.1% of requests and 1% of bots fail, and the summary reports any errors at the capacity it found. When the async queue sets the capacity, the summary suggests adding worker processes.
