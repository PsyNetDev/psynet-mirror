The `/node/<node_id>/fail`, `/info/<info_id>/fail`, `/trial/<trial_id>/fail` and `/network/<network_id>/grow` routes now require a dashboard login. Previously anyone who could reach the server could fail any node, info or trial, or grow any network, by guessing its sequential id. Scripts that call these routes must now send the dashboard credentials, for example with `requests.post(url, auth=(dashboard_user, dashboard_password))`.
