Added `SimpleGrouper(start_short_after=..., start_short_with=...)`, which starts a smaller group once the longest-waiting participant has waited `start_short_after` seconds and at least `start_short_with` participants are waiting. Such a group's minimum size is lowered to `start_short_with` if `min_group_size` is higher, so that it isn't dissolved at its first barrier. A `{SECONDS}` placeholder in `waiting_message` counts down to that start.
