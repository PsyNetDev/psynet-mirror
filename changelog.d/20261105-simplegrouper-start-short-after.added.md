Added `SimpleGrouper(start_short_after=..., start_short_with=...)`, which starts a smaller group once the longest-waiting participant has waited `start_short_after` seconds and at least `start_short_with` participants are waiting. `start_short_with` defaults to the grouper's `quorum`, and `quorum` defaults to `start_short_with` and may not be larger, so that a group started short isn't below quorum at its first barrier. A `{SECONDS}` placeholder in `waiting_message` counts down to that start.
