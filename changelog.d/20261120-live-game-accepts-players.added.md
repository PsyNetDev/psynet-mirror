Added `LiveGame.accepts_players(state)`, so that a live game with `join_running_game=True` can stop taking new players, such as once its first round starts. PsyNet then stops the group's top-ups. A newcomer the group admitted just before is never seen by the game: their game ends at once with the reason `TOO_LATE` (`"too_late"`), and their page shows the page's new `too_late_message` with the Next button. A newcomer who reaches a game just as it ends, before Dallinger could add them, comes too late in the same way.
