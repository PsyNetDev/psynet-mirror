A member removed from their group by a `GroupBarrier` with `late_action="leave_group"` now sees "Your partner went on without you. Please continue." or "Your group went on without you. Please continue." on the progress bar until they leave their page, even when their removal dissolves the group. Several late members removed at once are all recorded as late. This is independent of `notify_arrivals`. Customize it with `on_arrival_message` (`kind="removed"`), or return `None` there to hide it. A member removed with `max_wait_action="leave_group"` after waiting too long sees "Your partner didn't arrive in time. Please continue." (or "Not everyone in your group arrived in time. Please continue.") on the page they move on to (`kind="waited_too_long"`). Passing `on_arrival_message` together with `notify_arrivals=False` no longer turns arrival notices on, as it did in the 14.0 release candidates; drop `notify_arrivals=False` to keep them.
