A fixed group that plays a loop of cued rounds can now pass the loop value as `round=`, as in `Trial.cue({"question": i}, round=i)`. Each iteration is then the group's round of that number, so a round's definition can come from the loop: a player who joins the group late skips the rounds it finished without them (rounds whose `GroupBarrier` in `show_trial` has released the group: a cued round closes only then), a player who leaves plays the remaining iterations alone, and a player who leaves and rejoins the group skips a cue of a round in which they already have a trial of that class. A skipped round shows no pages. A module whose cues pass `round=` runs once per participant, and its cues raise an error if the participant has run the module before; give each run its own module id. Each member's group membership stores the cued round they last played or skipped: cueing a round with `round=` again after a later one, even a round the participant skipped, or a trial class again in a round they played, raises an error.
