A fixed group that plays a loop of cued rounds can now pass the loop value as `round=`, as in `Trial.cue({"question": i}, round=i)`. Each iteration is then the group's round of that number, so a round's definition can come from the loop: a player who joins the group late skips the rounds it finished without them (rounds whose `GroupBarrier` has released the group: a cued round closes only then, so put the barrier inside `show_trial`), and a player who leaves plays the remaining iterations alone. A module whose cues pass `round=` runs once per participant; give each run its own module id. Each member's group membership now stores the cued round they last played or skipped, and a `GroupBarrier` reached in or after a cued round, also between iterations or after the loop, waits only for the members who played that round and haven't passed this barrier in it yet, plus, while the round is still open, the members who haven't reached it. It doesn't wait for members who skipped the round, are in a round the group played after it, or are behind a round that has closed, and it releases the round's skippers with the players, or alone once the players have passed. So `on_release`'s `participants` and `trials` may now be a subset of the group's active members, and `leader` may not be among them.
