The design-simulation guidance (`power-analysis` and `precision-estimation` skills) now states the default criterion as a margin of error in the response's own units, such as rating points, with one threshold for all scenarios, and asks for the smallest adequate design under each assumption set. For profiles it adds the margin of error of a difference, the profile correlation and the smallest true spread at which the design reaches a 0.9 correlation, and it asks the analysis to report the achieved margin of error and split-half reliability after data collection.
