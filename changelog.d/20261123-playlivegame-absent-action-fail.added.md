`PlayLiveGame` accepts `absent_action="fail"`, which fails a player who doesn't play the game (a no-show, or a player away for the game's `away_timeout`) once the game's result is saved, as `late_action="fail"` fails a late player. The failure is tagged `live_game:<id>`, `fail_on_timeout` and `no_show` or `left_game`, the player keeps what the game paid them, and their groups follow their `below_quorum`, which, unlike with `absent_action="leave_group"`, may be `"fail"`.
