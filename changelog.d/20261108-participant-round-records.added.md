Added per-round records for group games. With `GroupBarrier(record_rounds=True)`, PsyNet writes, each time the barrier releases the group or the group dissolves mid-round, a `SyncGroupRound` row for the group and a `ParticipantRound` row for every member, including members who left during the round and players a `Regrouper` sat out. `on_release` and `on_dissolve` add each player's results with `barrier.record(participant, ..., bonus=...)`, which also pays the bonus so the record and the payment agree, and the group's results, such as a winning number or an offer, once with `barrier.record_group(...)`. `psynet.sync.participant_rounds(barrier_id, participant, round)` and `psynet.sync.group_rounds(barrier_id, participant, round)` return the two tables for `get_basic_data`, joined by `round_id`, or one participant's or one round's rows, for example to show a player how their latest round went. In groups a `Regrouper` forms, the round number is shared across the cohort. The `ultimatum_pairing` and `lowest_unique_number` demos use them.
