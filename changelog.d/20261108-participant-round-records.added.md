Added per-round records for group games. With `GroupBarrier(record_rounds=True)`, PsyNet writes a `ParticipantRound` row for every member each time the barrier releases the group, including members who left during the round and players a `Regrouper` sat out, and for every member when the group dissolves mid-round. `on_release` and `on_dissolve` add results with `participant.record_round(..., bonus=...)`, which also pays the bonus so the record and the payment agree, and `psynet.sync.participant_rounds(barrier_id, participant, round)` returns the rows for `get_basic_data`, or one participant's or one round's rows, for example to show a player their latest result. In groups a `Regrouper` forms, the round number is shared across the cohort. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo uses it in place of rebuilding rounds from response timestamps.
