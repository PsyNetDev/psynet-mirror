A `GroupBarrier`'s `on_release` can now take `left`, the members who left the group since its previous barrier pass, so a callback can tell a player whether their partner returned the study or was removed for being late (`participant.sync_group_removal_reason(group_type)`). `SyncGroup.members_who_left_since(time)` returns the same list, and each group membership records when it ended. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo uses it to tell players whose partner was away.
