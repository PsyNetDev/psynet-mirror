Added group rounds for cued trials: a trial class with `sync_group_type = "room"`, cued with `PickTrial.cue(definition)`, records the participant's group, role and round, and all the trials that a group's members cue in one module and round, whatever their class, share one `SyncGroupNode`, whose `var` holds the group's results. `cue` numbers each group's rounds from 0 by itself: a cue joins the group's newest node, or starts its next round if the participant already has a trial of that class there, so a `Regrouper`'s groups play round 0, a fixed group advances round by round, and a member who joins late joins the current round; `round=` overrides this in the rare cases that need it. The round's first cue sets the node's definition, and a later cue with a different `dict` raises an error. `definition` can be a function of `group` and `round`, called once per group and round. `GroupBarrier` `on_release` and `on_dissolve` callbacks can now take `trials`, the members' current trials, and `node`, the group's node for the round when those trials are on it, so a `@classmethod` on the trial class can score the round. The `lowest_unique_number` and `ultimatum_pairing` demos now play their rounds this way and build their basic data from the trials and nodes, and `lowest_unique_number` pays the winner a bonus and numbers each player's own rounds in its export. "Writing a round-based experiment" now builds the ultimatum game with cued trials.
