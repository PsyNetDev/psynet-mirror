Added group rounds for cued trials: a trial class with `sync_group_type = "room"`, cued with `PickTrial.cue(definition, round=number)`, records the participant's group, role and round, and the trials that a group's members cue for the same round share one `SyncGroupNode`, whose `var` holds the group's results. Pass `round` for a fixed group that plays several rounds. For groups re-formed every round, such as a `Regrouper`'s, whose members can be at different rounds, leave `round` out: the group's trials then share one node, with `sync_group_round` set to `None`, and cueing a second trial on it for the same participant raises an error asking for `round=`. `definition` can be a function of `group` and `round`, called once per group and round. `GroupBarrier` `on_release` and `on_dissolve` callbacks can now take `trials`, the members' current trials, and `node`, the node those trials share, so a `@classmethod` on the trial class can score the round. The `lowest_unique_number` and `ultimatum_pairing` demos now play their rounds this way and build their basic data from the trials and nodes, and `lowest_unique_number` pays the winner a bonus and numbers each player's own rounds in its export. "Writing a round-based experiment" now builds the ultimatum game with cued trials.
