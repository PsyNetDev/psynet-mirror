Added group rounds for cued trials: a trial class with `sync_group_type = "room"`, cued with `PickTrial.cue(definition)`, records the participant's group, role and round, and the trials that a group's members cue in one module and round share one `SyncGroupNode`, whose `var` holds the group's results. Without `round=`, `cue` numbers each group's rounds from 0 by itself: a cue joins the group's newest round while it is open and the participant has no trial there, and otherwise starts the next. A round is open until one of its `GroupBarrier`s releases the group, so a round needs a barrier inside `show_trial`, and building a grouped trial's pages raises an error if `show_trial` has none. A `Regrouper`'s groups use this automatic numbering and play round 0. A fixed group that plays a loop of rounds should pass the loop value as `round=`, so that each iteration is the group's round of that number and its definition can come from the loop; two trial classes played one after the other in one round also pass the same `round=`. The round's first cue sets the node's definition, and a later cue with a different `dict` raises an error. `definition` can be a function of `group` and `round`, called once per group and round (for ungrouped cues, once per trial). `GroupBarrier` `on_release` callbacks can now take `trials`, the members' current trials, and `node`, the group's node for the round when those trials are on it, so a `@classmethod` on the trial class can score the round. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo plays its rounds this way and builds its basic data from the trials and nodes. "Writing a round-based experiment" now builds the ultimatum game with cued trials.
