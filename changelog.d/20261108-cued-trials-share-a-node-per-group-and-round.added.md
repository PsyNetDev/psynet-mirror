Added group rounds for cued trials: a trial class with `sync_group_type = "room"`, cued with `PickTrial.cue(definition, round=number)`, records the participant's group, role and round, and the trials that a group's members cue for the same round share one `SyncGroupNode`, whose `var` holds the group's results. `definition` can be a function of `group` and `round`, called once per group and round. `GroupBarrier` `on_release` and `on_dissolve` callbacks can now take `trials`, the members' current trials, and `node`, the node those trials share, so a `@classmethod` on the trial class can score the round. The `lowest_unique_number` demo now plays its rounds this way, pays the winner a bonus and builds its basic data from the trials and nodes.
