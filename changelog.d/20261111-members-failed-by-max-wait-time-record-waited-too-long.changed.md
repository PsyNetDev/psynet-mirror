A member who waits at a `GroupBarrier` for longer than its `max_wait_time` now leaves the group as `"waited_too_long"` with the default `max_wait_action="fail"` too, before being failed, so `participant.sync_group_removal_reason(group_type)` and their cued trial's `sync_group_outcome` say `"waited_too_long"` instead of `"left_experiment"`. The absent member, left alone below the quorum, still gets `"group_dissolved"`. The failed member's other groups, such as a cohort they share with the absent member, still record `"left_experiment"`, and they see the same final page as before. A member who times out on a barrier's `waiting_logic` pages is now tagged `while_loop:barrier:<id>` whether their own request or the barrier poller times them out. Removal reasons and `sync_group_outcome` are new in this release (no earlier PsyNet release or release candidate recorded them), so nothing needs migrating.
