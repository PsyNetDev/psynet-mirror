`SimpleGrouper(min_group_size=...)` is deprecated and emits a `FutureWarning`; pass `quorum=...` instead, the number of active members a group needs to keep going. Sync groups gain a matching `quorum` attribute. A `SimpleGrouper` with groups of three or more and no `quorum` now warns, so that experiments decide how many members may drop out; pass `quorum=initial_group_size` to keep the old behavior.
