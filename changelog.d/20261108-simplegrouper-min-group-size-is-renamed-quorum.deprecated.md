`SimpleGrouper(min_group_size=...)` is deprecated and emits a `FutureWarning`; pass `quorum=...` instead, the number of active members a group needs to keep going. `SimpleSyncGroup` gains a matching `quorum` attribute. `min_group_size=0` is read as `quorum=1`. A `SimpleGrouper` with groups of three or more and no `quorum` now warns, so that experiments decide how many members may drop out; pass `quorum=initial_group_size` to keep the old behavior. A `quorum` larger than `max_group_size` raises an error, since such a group could never reach it.
