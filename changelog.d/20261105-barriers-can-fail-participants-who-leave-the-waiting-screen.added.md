Added `fail_if_hidden_for` to groupers and barriers: a participant whose tab is hidden or closed for that many seconds while waiting there is failed. This covers the built-in waiting screen, which warns them, and every page of `waiting_logic`, including pages inside trials and with `release_grace=None`, which now check in about every 2 seconds too. In multiplayer games this stops a player who left from being put into a group that then waits for them. The `lowest_unique_number` and `ultimatum_pairing` demos use `fail_if_hidden_for=15`.
