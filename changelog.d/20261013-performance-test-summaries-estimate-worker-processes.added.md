`psynet performance-test` summaries now estimate how many async worker processes (`worker_processes`) the suggested participant cap, or a single test's bot count, needs, and whether to set `worker_greenlets_per_process = 1`. The estimate comes from how busy the workers were in the steady state and from the start-up burst that the capacity search measured, and is limited by the server's CPU cores. When the summary suggests keeping the current processes, it says how many the load needs; when no test reached a steady state, it names a test longer than a session that would give an estimate. Summaries also warn, without failing the test, when the workers were more than 85% busy in the steady state. Each test's report shows how busy the workers were, and the `--json-output` results record `worker_load` and `server_cores`.
