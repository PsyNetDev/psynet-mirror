Added `Trial.time_limit_reached`, a stored column that says whether the trial's latest timed page ran out of time before it was answered. Later untimed pages, pages shown while waiting at a barrier and pages after the trial is complete leave it unchanged, and a timed page after a completed trial, such as a feedback notice whose time limit only moves it on, no longer makes `GroupPrompt` show the participant as having run out of time at the next barrier. Added `SyncGroup.rounds(module_id)`, which returns a group's nodes for a module of cued trials in round order, also after the group has ended.
