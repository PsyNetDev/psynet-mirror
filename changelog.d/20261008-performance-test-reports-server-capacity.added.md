Each `psynet performance-test` run's summary now reports the server's capacity: the largest tested number of bots that kept the 95th-percentile response time of `/timeline` and `/response`, together and for each of them, under `--max-p95-ms` (default 500), kept the 95th-percentile async-process queue wait under `--max-queue-p95-s` (default 5), counting processes still queued when the test ends, and had fewer than 0.1% of requests and 1% of bots fail. Once a larger count exceeded the limits, the summary names the limit it exceeded, or what made the bots fail (for example `HTTP 500 on /participant_status`), and suggests a `max_concurrent_participants` of 80% of the capacity; it also suggests adding worker processes when the async queue set the limit, and reports any errors in the test at the capacity it found. Tests whose bots didn't all start within the measurement window, or in which fewer than 90% of the requested bots were ever taking part at the same time, count as exceeding the limits, and async processes left queued by one test are given time to start before the next.
