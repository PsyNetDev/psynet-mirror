A member failed by `GroupBarrier(late_action="fail")` now leaves the barrier's group with the reason `"late"` before being failed, as with the other `late_action` values, so `participant.sync_group_removal_reason()` returns `"late"` for that group instead of `"left_experiment"`. When several members are late, all of them leave and are failed before the group's quorum is checked, so none of them is removed as `"group_dissolved"` or failed for the group falling below its quorum.
