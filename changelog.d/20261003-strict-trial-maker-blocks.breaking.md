Made trial maker blocks strict: participants only receive nodes or chains from their current block, and stay in it until `max_trials_per_block` or `should_finish_block` ends it or it runs out. When every candidate in the block is busy, `wait_for_networks=True` waits and `False` finishes the block early. `should_finish_block` now takes `(participant, block)`, `module_state.block` is read-only, `set_block_state` has been removed, and an empty list from `find_chains` or `find_nodes` finishes the block instead of the trial maker.
