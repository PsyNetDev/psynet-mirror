`GroupBarrier` now tells two kinds of visit apart, stored when each member arrives. A barrier inside a cued round's trial (in `show_trial` of a trial class with `sync_group_type`) is a round visit: it waits for the members with a trial in that round and, while the round is open, for those who haven't cued yet; its release closes the round, and `on_release` gets the round's `node`. Any other barrier, such as one before the first cue, in `show_feedback`, at the start of each iteration or of a later run of the module, or after the loop, is a group visit: it waits for every member still in the module, gets `node=None`, and `trials` holds the members' current trials. The two kinds never wait for each other, neither waits for members who have finished the module, and every release except a member's own time-out is the group's pass, which runs `on_release` and counts for `left`, `late_after` and `pass_newcomers`. A late joiner of a fixed group's loop therefore catches up only through barriers in `show_trial`: a group barrier inside the loop, such as one at the start of each iteration, waits for the newcomer's partners while they wait in the round for the newcomer, until `late_after` or `max_wait_time` moves someone on. PsyNet logs a warning naming both barriers when this happens; move the barrier into `show_trial`, or stop top-ups with `stop_top_ups=True` on a barrier before the module.
