`PushButtonControl`, `RadioButtonControl`, `DropdownControl` and `CheckboxControl` now reject a submitted answer that isn't one of their choices, showing "Please choose one of the options." instead of saving it, so a tampered request can't reach code that expects a valid choice. This check also runs when the page has its own `validate` function. Empty selections, radio free text and bots that submit a formatted `answer` are still accepted.
