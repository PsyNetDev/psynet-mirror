A `GroupBarrier` waits for every active member of its group, wherever it is: in `show_trial`, in `show_feedback`, between iterations, between runs of a looped module or after the loop. A member stops being waited for only by leaving the group, with `leave_group` or by being removed (for example as late), so a member whose loop ends earlier should leave the group first. Pages between rounds or runs, inside the module or outside it, need nothing special: the next barrier waits for a member who reads slowly. A release closes a cued round when every member it releases is in that round's trial. Every release except a member's own time-out is the group's pass, which runs `on_release` and counts for `left`, `late_after` and `pass_newcomers`. When every member of a group waits at one of its barriers but no barrier has them all, so none can pass until `late_after` or `max_wait_time` moves someone on, the background barrier check records one error for the group naming the barriers and who waits at each, however the members got there, logs it and lists it under the dashboard's Errors tab; a bot test run by `psynet test local` then fails with `GroupDivergenceError` and that message when it ends. The usual cause is a newcomer who joins a fixed group during a loop of rounds and waits at a barrier outside the round's trial while the others wait for them in the round, so stop top-ups before such a game with `stop_top_ups=True`.
