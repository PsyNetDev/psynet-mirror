`GroupBarrier(late_action="leave_group")` no longer removes a late member while their own request is in progress, for example while they submit a page; like `late_action="fail"`, it reconsiders them at the barrier's next check. Both actions now also recheck lateness once the member's row is locked, so a member who reaches the barrier during the check is not removed as late. Code that runs in a late member's own request, such as a `CodeBlock` storing their answer, no longer needs a row lock to avoid racing the removal.
