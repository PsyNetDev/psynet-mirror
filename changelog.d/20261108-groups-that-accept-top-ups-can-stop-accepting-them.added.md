Added `SimpleSyncGroup.stop_top_ups()`, which stops newcomers joining a group formed with `join_existing_groups=True` at once, for example once its game has started. A group already below its quorum is dissolved straight away; from then on the group is treated as one without top-ups, so `below_quorum` can now be combined with `join_existing_groups` (see "Groups below quorum" in the synchronization docs).
