`psynet performance-test` now reports, for each chain trial maker, how many bots got fewer trials because every chain was busy and `wait_for_networks=False` finished their block early, with their median number of trials. More than 1% of bots started in a trial maker counts against capacity (one such bot per trial maker is always allowed), and the summary then suggests a participant cap alongside design remedies such as `wait_for_networks=True` or more chains. Chain trial makers record these blocks in the participant's trial maker state as `blocks_ended_busy`, leaving out chains busy only with their last trials; participants are not failed.
