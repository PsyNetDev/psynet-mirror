Added the `public_goods` demo, a repeated public goods game in groups of three that carries on when members return the study or stop responding and, when one player is left, cancels the round and lets them keep their tokens for it. Each round is one cued trial per player (`Trial.cue(round=i)`), which pays that round's bonus, with the group's pot saved once on the node the group's trials share and each player's `sync_group_outcome` in the export.
