Added `waiting_screen` to barriers and groupers: a function of the participant that returns a prompt, text or page to show in place of their last page while they wait, with the waiting message still on top. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo uses it to show each player's choice while their partner decides, and to replace the instructions and results while teams and pairs form. Its decision page now also counts down from 30 seconds, and a player who runs out of time gets nothing that round, and nor does their partner.
