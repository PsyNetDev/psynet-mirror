Added a confirmation test to the end of `psynet performance-test --n-bots auto`: it runs the suggested participant cap with bots arriving over one session, for two sessions (at most an hour), and the summary reports the cap as confirmed, or as not confirmed with the limits the start-up and steady state exceeded and a lower cap to check. Skip it with `--no-confirm`; it never runs with `--time-factor 0`. The `--json-output` results now record each test's `arrival_minutes`, `session_minutes` and, for the confirmation test, `confirmation`, the number of async processes in each phase, and the options record `confirmation_skipped`.
