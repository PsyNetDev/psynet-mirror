A participant whose arrival at a barrier with `waiting_logic` releases them at once, such as the one who completes a group, normally no longer starts the waiting logic, so they no longer get a filler trial that was never shown and was failed with `failed_reason="interrupted_by_release"`. Participants who arrive at the same moment don't see each other's arrivals yet, so each of them can still get such a trial, which is not paid.
