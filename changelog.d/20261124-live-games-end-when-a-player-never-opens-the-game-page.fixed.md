Fixed live games carrying on when a player never opens the game page: a player who isn't ready when the game starts now counts as away from the start, so the game's `away_timeout` ends a game left with too few players, and `PlayLiveGame(absent_action="fail")` fails them as a no-show. In the `rock_paper_scissors_live` demo, such a game now ends within a minute, and the partner sees their result before the absent player is failed as late at the next game. The `public_goods_live` demo fails such players with `absent_action="fail"`, leaves them out of the pot and the round's pay, and shows them to the other players as not here. Both demos start a game without them sooner.
