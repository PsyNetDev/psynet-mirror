Fixed live games carrying on when a player never opens the game page: a player who isn't ready when the game starts now counts as away from the start, so the game's `away_timeout` ends a game left with too few players, and `PlayLiveGame(absent_action="fail")` fails them as a no-show. The `rock_paper_scissors_live` and `public_goods_live` demos now fail such players with `absent_action="fail"` and start a game without them sooner, and in `public_goods_live` they no longer share the pot or get paid for the round, and the other players' pages show them as not here.
