Fixed live games carrying on until their `timeout` or `duration` when a player never opens the game page. A player who isn't ready when the game starts now counts as away from the start, so the game's `away_timeout` ends a game left with too few players promptly, and `PlayLiveGame(absent_action="fail")` then fails them as a no-show without waiting for the game's time limit. Once the game has started, such players are also in `state["psynet"]["away"]` (as `on_start` and `onPlayersAway` see it), and the other players' pages show the `player_left_message` notice for them. In the `rock_paper_scissors_live` demo, such a game now ends within a minute, and the partner sees their result before the absent player is failed as late at the next game. The `public_goods_live` demo fails such players with `absent_action="fail"`, leaves them out of the pot and the round's pay, marks them in its export's new `arrived` column, shows them to the other players as not here, and ends a round as it starts, without a pot, when fewer than two players open it (marked by its export's new `end_reason` column). Both demos start a game without them sooner.
