Fixed live games carrying on until their `timeout` or `duration` when a player never opens the game page. A player who isn't ready when the game starts without them now counts as away from the start, for the game's `away_timeout`, and `PlayLiveGame(absent_action="fail")` fails them as a no-show without waiting for the game's time limit. Once the game has started, such players are in `state["psynet"]["away"]`, and the other players' pages show the `player_left_message` notice for them.
