Tidied the multiplayer demos to use public interfaces and store results rather than infer them: `create_rate_sync` scores each phase from `trial.answer` in a barrier's `on_release` and keeps the results in `trial.var`, `ultimatum_pairing` stores each pair's `proposer_id` and `responder_id` on its node, its per-player export table is now `player_round`, and the demos name their group types with constants (`GROUP`, or `COHORT` and `PAIR` in `ultimatum_pairing`).
