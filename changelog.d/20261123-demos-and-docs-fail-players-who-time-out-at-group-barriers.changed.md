The `public_goods`, `ultimatum_pairing`, `microsociety`, `story_chain` and `crowd_estimate` demos, the multiplayer docs and the `synchronous-experiments` Agent Skill now recommend failing players who time out at a group barrier (`late_action="fail"`), while their teammates carry on where the design allows: a regrouper re-pairs the partner at the next split, and a fixed group plays on down to its quorum (`below_quorum="fail"`). Failed players are paid for what they completed. `crowd_estimate` no longer lets players without a group answer alone, and a new docs section, "What the remaining players see when someone times out", shows how a page after a barrier handles a partner's missing input. `late_action="leave_group"`, `below_quorum="continue_without_group"` and `sync_group_optional` remain supported as advanced options. Rerun `psynet scripts update` in experiments to get the updated skill.
