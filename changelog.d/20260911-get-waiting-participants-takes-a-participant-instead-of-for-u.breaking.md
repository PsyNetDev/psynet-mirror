``Barrier.get_waiting_participants()`` now takes a participant as the first argument instead of ``for_update``. Pass ``for_update`` as a keyword. Grouped barriers require a participant or the visit link: ``participant.active_barriers[id].get_waiting_participants()`` or ``barrier.get_waiting_participants(participant)``. Ungrouped groupers can still list the active pool without a participant. ``Barrier.get_waiting_participants_from_barrier_id`` was removed.
