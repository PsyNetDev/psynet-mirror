Deprecated the label argument of `conditional`, `switch`, `while_loop`, `sequence` and `randomize`. Write `conditional(condition, logic_if_true)` instead of `conditional("label", condition, logic_if_true)`; the old form still works but warns. `label=` remains available as a keyword for `conditional`, `switch` and `while_loop`, where the branch log now defaults to the name of the condition function, and is required on a `while_loop` with `max_loop_time`. `sequence` and `randomize` ignore it. `for_loop` still requires its label.
