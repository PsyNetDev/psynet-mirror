The background barrier check no longer re-checks, at every sweep, a waiting group whose release can't be due yet. A built-in barrier's check now says when its release could next be due, for example when a member becomes late or a `Regrouper` deadline comes, and the background check leaves the group's visit until then or until a member's state changes, re-checking it every 10 seconds. If that re-check releases anyone, the release came late, and a warning in the logs names the barrier. Participants don't wait any longer: a group's last arrival still releases it at once. With many waiting groups, time-based releases such as `late_after` therefore no longer wait for a pass over every group. Barrier subclasses that override `choose_who_to_release`, `check_waiting_participants`, `ready_to_group` or `group` are still checked at every sweep.
