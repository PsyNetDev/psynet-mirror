The background barrier check no longer re-checks, at every sweep, a waiting group whose release can't be due yet. A built-in barrier's check now says when its release could next be due, for example when a member becomes late or a `Regrouper` deadline comes, and the group waits until then or until a member's state changes, with a re-check every 10 seconds. With many waiting groups, time-based releases such as `late_after` therefore no longer wait for a pass over every group. Barrier subclasses that override `choose_who_to_release`, `check_waiting_participants` or `ready_to_group` are still checked at every sweep.
