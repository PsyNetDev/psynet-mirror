Added `participant.sync_group_removal_reason(group_type)`, which says why a participant left their group: `"late"`, `"waited_too_long"`, `"no_group_formed"` (a grouper found them no group in time), `"group_dissolved"`, `"moved_on"`, `"finished"`, `"left_experiment"` (failed, including returning the study) or `"removed"` (removed from the dashboard, or by `SyncGroup.remove_participant` without another reason). `"late"` is recorded with every `GroupBarrier` `late_action`: with `late_action="fail"`, the member leaves the barrier's group as `"late"`, and their other groups as `"left_experiment"`, before being failed. Members who are late together are all removed and failed at once, before the group's quorum is checked, so none of them is removed as `"group_dissolved"`. Every member still in a group when it dissolves below its quorum leaves it as `"group_dissolved"`, with any `below_quorum`; with `below_quorum="fail"`, they are then failed and leave their other groups as `"left_experiment"`. Answers submitted on the page where a member was removed for being late have `response.too_late_for_group` set, so analyses can leave them out.
