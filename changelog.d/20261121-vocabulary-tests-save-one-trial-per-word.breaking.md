WikiVocab and BibleVocab now save one trial per word shown, instead of one trial per form. Each word is an ordinary trial page that shares its HTML with the others, and the words are prepared in advance, so the next word appears without waiting for the server. The constructors still take the number of forms, their size and the per-form screening thresholds; each form is checked once its last word is answered, and a participant who fails it stops there. A word left unanswered until the response timeout scores its form 0, as does a form left unfinished. In new exports, read the participant's real or fake choice from each trial's `answer` and the response time in seconds from `time_taken`. Within each trial maker, `position` gives the order of presentation and trials with the same `node_id` are repeats of the same word. Data collected with earlier versions keep their per-form answer lists. `VocabPage` is removed, as are `VocabTest`'s per-form methods (`score_trial`, `score_accuracy`, `score_consistency`, `choose_hashes`, `select_hashes` and `get_assets`) and `VocabTrial`'s (`items`, `finalize_definition`, `get_base_test_config`, `show_image_trial` and `show_text_trial`); `image_setup` no longer takes the concatenated characters. To change scoring, override `VocabTest.score_form`, and to change the page, `VocabTest.word_page`. Word images are now generated on demand from node assets, so exports no longer include them. The old page ignored `hide_after`, `lag_between_items` and the key codes, always showing each word for 1 second, 200 ms apart, with the A and L keys; these settings now take effect, and `hide_after` defaults to 1000 ms to match what participants saw before.
