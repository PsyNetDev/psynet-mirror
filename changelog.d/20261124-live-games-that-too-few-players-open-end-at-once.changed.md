Changed live games so that a game which fewer than its `min_players` players have opened by its `ready_timeout` ends at once, without starting, with the new reason `TOO_FEW_READY` (`"too_few_ready"`), instead of starting and waiting a further `away_timeout`. This also replaces Dallinger's `"not_joined"` for a game that nobody opened. `absences()` still reports the players who didn't open it as `"no_show"`, so `PlayLiveGame(absent_action="fail")` and rewards work as before, the game's `on_start` doesn't run, and the pages of the players who did open it show the page's `no_game_message`. A game that should carry on short-handed sets a lower `min_players`. The `bargaining_live`, `public_goods_live` and `rock_paper_scissors_live` demos rely on this, so `rock_paper_scissors_live` now ends a game at once when the partner never opens it, and the export's `end_reason` for such a round in `bargaining_live` and `public_goods_live` is `"too_few_ready"`.
