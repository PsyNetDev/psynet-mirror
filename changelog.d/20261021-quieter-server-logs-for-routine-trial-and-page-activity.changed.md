The server log is much quieter: routine messages about trial selection, trial finalization, rewards, timeline loops and holds, received responses, browser-side `info` messages (`[CLIENT n]`) and the details of each recruitment check (`Target number of participants = …`, `Recruitment criterion …`) are now logged at DEBUG instead of INFO. The `GET /timeline` and `POST /response` timing lines and the `Task '…' took … s` lines appear at INFO only for requests and tasks that take at least 1 second. Warnings, errors and recruitment decisions (`Conclusion: …`) still appear at INFO. Messages from Dallinger's `Experiment.log`, such as recruiter notifications, now go through the `psynet` logger instead of being printed with a `>>>>` prefix. `psynet debug`, `psynet test` and `psynet deploy` report healthy local PostgreSQL and Redis in one line, and the "Creating networks" and "Staging assets" progress bars are only shown in a terminal, not in redirected output. To see the DEBUG messages again, add `logging.getLogger("psynet").setLevel(logging.DEBUG)` to your `experiment.py`.
