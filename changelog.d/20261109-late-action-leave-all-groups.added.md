Added `late_action="leave_all_groups"` to `GroupBarrier` (and `sync_group_late_action` to trial makers): a late member leaves all their groups, for example the cohort their pair was formed in as well as the pair, so the cohort's next split doesn't wait for them. PsyNet warns, when the experiment loads and when it happens, if that would take a group without top-ups below its quorum and so fail its other members. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo uses it instead of removing late players from the cohort by hand.
