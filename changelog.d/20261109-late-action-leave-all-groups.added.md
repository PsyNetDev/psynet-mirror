Added `late_action="leave_all_groups"` to `GroupBarrier` (and `sync_group_late_action` to trial makers): a late member leaves all their groups, for example the cohort their pair was formed in as well as the pair, so the cohort's next split doesn't wait for them. PsyNet logs a warning when that takes a group without top-ups below its quorum and so fails its other members (unless its grouper has `below_quorum="continue_without_group"`). The `ultimatum_pairing` demo uses it instead of removing late players from the cohort by hand.
