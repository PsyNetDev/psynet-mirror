Added optional camera and screen background recording alongside ordinary page answers in in-place LocalStorage experiments, with a Recordings dashboard and recording outcomes in the asset manifest. Set `VideoRecordConfig(required=True)` within a trial to require its clips: the trial cannot finalize, and its feedback waits, until they arrive, and it fails if a clip is unavailable or still missing at its upload deadline. Optional clips never hold a page. Capture stops at its duration or size limit and keeps what was recorded. Later required pages offer another permission decision if capture was denied or stopped.
