Added camera and screen background recording to timeline pages, including browser-hosted jsPsych and Unity, preserving their answers while clips upload across page changes. Capture requires an explicit browser permission step, respects stored audiovisual consent, and has duration and memory limits. Clips are optional by default. Authors can opt into `VideoRecordConfig(required=True)` within a trial to hold finalization and fail the affected trial if a clip is missing at its upload deadline, without retrying capture or blocking unrelated answer analysis. Permission decisions precede task startup, including Unity loader startup, and persistent sessions create a separate clip for each logical page. A Recordings dashboard and asset-manifest columns expose capture/upload outcomes and preserve unavailable recordings without broken download links. Includes a comparison demo and manual testing instructions.
