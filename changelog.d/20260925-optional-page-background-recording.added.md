Added camera and screen background recording to ordinary timeline pages, preserving their answers while clips upload across page changes. Capture requires an explicit browser permission step, respects stored audiovisual consent, and has duration and memory limits. Clips are optional by default. Authors can opt into `VideoRecordConfig(required=True)` within a trial to hold finalization and fail the affected trial if a clip is missing at its upload deadline, without retrying capture or blocking unrelated answer analysis. Includes a comparison demo and manual testing instructions.
