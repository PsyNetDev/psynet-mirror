Fixed participants who close the page while waiting at a grouper or barrier never being timed out, and the background barrier check slowing down in proportion to the number of waiting participants. With a few thousand of them it took over 20 seconds per run instead of its 0.5-second interval, keeping one CPU core busy. The background check now applies `max_wait_time` to waiters whose deadline has passed, failing or kicking them as `max_wait_action` says, so their partners are handled as if they had timed out in the browser. It also spreads its re-checks of unchanged waiting groups over several runs, so each run takes a fraction of a second however many participants are waiting.
