`psynet export ssh` now exports apps deployed without `--app`, which are served at the server's own (root) domain. `--app` accepts their `dlgr-...` name as listed by `psynet apps ssh`, and a full experiment UUID suggests that name. When neither a local launch note nor the server's deployment record gives the app's address, the export reads it from the server's Caddy routes instead of assuming `https://<app>.<server>`.
