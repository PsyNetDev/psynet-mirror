When a page comes straight after the current page in the timeline, PsyNet now renders it in advance, so it appears as soon as the participant clicks Next while the answer is sent in the background. The browser still waits for the server after pages that may turn down an answer, such as built-in radio buttons that ask for a missing answer. Pass `render_in_advance=False` to a page whose content depends on the previous answer; this also applies to trial pages that a trial maker prepares in advance. See "Showing the next page without waiting" in the timeline documentation.
