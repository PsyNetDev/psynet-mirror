Added `psynet.sync.only_in_group(group_type, *steps)`, which skips the remaining steps of a round once a participant is no longer in their group, instead of a `conditional` before every page. Lists and `join(...)` are split into their steps, so `only_in_group("pair", [round_steps(n) for n in range(N_ROUNDS)])` checks the group before every step of every round, while a barrier, grouper, `AsyncCodeBlock`, `conditional` or loop counts as one step. The grouper that forms the group goes before `only_in_group`, not among its steps. Its `otherwise=` runs after the steps for participants who are not in the group by then, including those who never had one, for example to answer the rest of the question alone.
