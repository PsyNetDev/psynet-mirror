`psynet performance-test` JSON results no longer include `avg_response_time`, `p99_response_time`, `stddev_response_time`, `p95_wait_page_time`, `max_wait_page_time`, `avg_failed_duration`, `avg_incomplete_duration`, `avg_init_time`, `initialization_times`, `n_succeeded_bots` (the same as `bots_succeeded`) or `completed_during_test`. The per-test report no longer shows bot start-up times, a completion rate, or the mean, standard deviation and 99th percentile of response times, and shows the median time on wait pages per bot without its 95th percentile and maximum.
