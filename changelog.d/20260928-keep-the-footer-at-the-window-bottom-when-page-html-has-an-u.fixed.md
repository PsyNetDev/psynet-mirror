Fixed page layout when page HTML contains an unclosed tag such as `<b>`: the footer no longer floats up to just below the content, a footerless page's media-download bar stays at the bottom of the window, and dialogs no longer inherit the stray formatting. PsyNet now moves this page furniture back into place as the page loads and logs a warning naming the unclosed tag. The unclosed tag in the `REPPVolumeCalibration` pre-screen (and its tapping demo copy) is also fixed.
