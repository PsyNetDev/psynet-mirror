`PushButtonControl`, `KeyboardPushButtonControl`, `RadioButtonControl`, `DropdownControl` and `CheckboxControl` now reject an answer that is not one of their choices, such as an out-of-range value from a tampered request, even when the control or page has its own `validate`. Empty answers and the free-text option of `RadioButtonControl` are still accepted. A subclass whose `format_answer` saves something other than a choice must pass `restrict_to_choices=False`.
