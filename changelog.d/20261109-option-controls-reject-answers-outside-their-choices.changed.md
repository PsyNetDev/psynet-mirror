`PushButtonControl`, `KeyboardPushButtonControl`, `RadioButtonControl`, `DropdownControl` and `CheckboxControl` now reject a submitted answer that is not one of their choices, such as an out-of-range value from a tampered request or a bot `bot_response` of the wrong type (`"3"` for the choice `3`). The check runs before any `validate`, so a control's or page's own `validate`, including a page that overrides `validate` like the demography `CountryOfBirth` page, cannot accept such an answer. Empty answers such as `None` still pass this check and are left to `force_selection`, and the free-text option of `RadioButtonControl` accepts any text. `CheckboxControl(force_selection=True)` now rejects a `None` answer instead of raising an error. If a control or page overrides `format_answer` to save something other than a choice, such as a dictionary of the choice and a reaction time, pass `restrict_to_choices=False` to the control; `TimedPushButtonControl` and its subclasses do this by default.
