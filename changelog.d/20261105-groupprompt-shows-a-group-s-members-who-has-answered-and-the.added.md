Added `psynet.group_prompt.GroupPrompt`, which shows the members of a group as their pseudonym animals drawn in their colours, in a row or a zigzag across the page and optionally clustered into subgroups, ticks members off live as they answer (in place of the group arrival notice), with a grey dash instead for members whose page's time limit ran out, and shows members who join or leave, and then shows everyone's answers in speech bubbles, with optional highlights such as the winner. A page shows either progress (`show_progress`, on by default without `answers`) or `answers`, and contradictory combinations raise an error. `has_answered` replaces the default check, `psynet.sync.is_waiting_at_barrier`, and members waiting after a time-out (`psynet.sync.reached_barrier_after_time_limit`) keep their dash whatever it returns. `names=` chooses how members are named: `"on_hover"` (default), `"shown"` below each animal, or `"anonymous"`, which shows every member as the same neutral figure. Joining their first sync group now gives a newcomer a different pseudonym if an active member already has their derived one (`PseudonymScheme.assign_distinct` chooses it); pseudonyms set with `Participant.set_pseudonym` are kept. `PseudonymScheme.render_sticker` draws these animals, using bundled Microsoft Fluent Emoji pictures (MIT licence) for the built-in animals. The synchronous demos (`chatroom_simple`, `create_rate_sync`, `gibbs_within_sync`, `lowest_unique_number`, `rock_paper_scissors`, `simple_sync_group`, `sync_quorum` and `ultimatum_pairing`) show their groups with it.
