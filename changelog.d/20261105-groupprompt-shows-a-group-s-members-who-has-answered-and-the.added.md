Added `psynet.group_prompt.GroupPrompt`, which shows the members of a group as their pseudonym animals tinted in their colours, in a row or scattered over the page and optionally clustered into subgroups, ticks members off live as they answer, and then shows everyone's answers in speech bubbles, with optional highlights such as the winner. Joining a sync group now gives a newcomer a different pseudonym if an active member already has theirs (`PseudonymScheme.replacement` chooses it). `PseudonymScheme.sticker_html` draws these tinted animals, using bundled Microsoft Fluent Emoji pictures (MIT licence) for the built-in animals.
