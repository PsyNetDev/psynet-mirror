Added `psynet.group_prompt.GroupPrompt`, which shows the members of a group as silhouettes of their pseudonym animals, in a row or scattered over the page and optionally clustered into subgroups, ticks members off live as they answer, and then shows everyone's answers in speech bubbles, with optional highlights such as the winner. Joining a sync group now gives a newcomer a different pseudonym if an active member already has theirs (`PseudonymScheme.replacement` chooses it).
