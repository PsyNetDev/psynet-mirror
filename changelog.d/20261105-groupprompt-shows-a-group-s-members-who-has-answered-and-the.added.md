Added `psynet.group_prompt.GroupPrompt`, which shows the members of a group as their pseudonym animals drawn in their colours, in a row or a zigzag across the page and optionally clustered into subgroups, ticks members off live as they answer and shows members who join or leave, and then shows everyone's answers in speech bubbles, with optional highlights such as the winner. `anonymous=True` shows every member as the same neutral figure instead. Joining their first sync group now gives a newcomer a different pseudonym if an active member already has theirs (`PseudonymScheme.replacement` chooses it). `PseudonymScheme.sticker_html` draws these animals, using bundled Microsoft Fluent Emoji pictures (MIT licence) for the built-in animals.
