Group barriers now show a pill on the progress bar when a partner is already waiting (``Your partner is ready.``, or ``{ARRIVED}/{TOTAL} of your group are ready.``). Waiting participants in groups of three or more see remaining-not-ready copy on the hold overlay (``{REMAINING} of {TOTAL} not ready yet``); pairs keep the hold title only. Longer notice copy ellipsizes instead of wrapping over the prompt. Set ``notify_arrivals=False`` to disable notices, or pass ``on_arrival_message`` to customize the copy.
