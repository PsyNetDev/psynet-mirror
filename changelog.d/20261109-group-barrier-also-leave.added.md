Added `also_leave` to `GroupBarrier` (and `sync_group_also_leave` to trial makers): with `late_action="leave_group"`, it lists the other group types a late member leaves too, for example `also_leave=["cohort"]` for the cohort their pair was formed in, so the cohort's next split doesn't wait for them. Their groups of other types keep them. PsyNet logs a warning when this takes a group without top-ups below its quorum and so fails its other members (unless its grouper has `below_quorum="continue_without_group"`). The `ultimatum_pairing` demo uses it instead of removing late players from the cohort by hand.
