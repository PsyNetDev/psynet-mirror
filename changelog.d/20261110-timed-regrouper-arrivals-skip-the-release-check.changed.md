Participants who arrive at a `Regrouper` with `every` by pressing Next no longer wait for a check of the whole waiting pool, since an arrival cannot make the pool ready; the background barrier check makes each split at its next check after the tick, normally within a second. An arrival or reload while loading the timeline page still checks the waiter's visit. This makes large regrouped experiments faster under load. Barrier checks now log only the number of waiting participants at INFO and their IDs at DEBUG, and the troubleshooting docs explain that PostgreSQL's `could not obtain lock on row` errors in group experiments are expected.
