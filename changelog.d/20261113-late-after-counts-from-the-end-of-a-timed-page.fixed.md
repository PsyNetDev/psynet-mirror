Fixed a group barrier's `late_after` giving a member only 10 seconds after a page with a `time_limit` before counting them as late, so a member reading a short page after a timed one could be removed from their group. As its documentation says, `late_after` now counts from when the timed page ends (plus the 10 seconds an answer sent at the deadline needs to arrive), so it needs to cover only the pages without a time limit. Experiments that raised `late_after` to cover their timed pages can lower it.
