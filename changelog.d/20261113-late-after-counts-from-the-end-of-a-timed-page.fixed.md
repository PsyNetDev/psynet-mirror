Fixed a group barrier's `late_after` giving a member only 10 seconds after a page with a `time_limit` before counting them as late, so a member reading a short page after a timed one could be removed from their group. As its documentation says, `late_after` now counts from when the timed page ends, plus the 10 seconds an answer sent at the deadline needs to arrive, so it needs to cover only the pages without a time limit. A member who closes their tab on a timed page is therefore removed later than before: with a 30-second page and `late_after=30`, after about 70 seconds rather than 40. Experiments that sized `late_after` to cover their timed pages should lower it; the `story_chain`, `microsociety` and `ultimatum_pairing` demos now use 15 to 30 seconds.
