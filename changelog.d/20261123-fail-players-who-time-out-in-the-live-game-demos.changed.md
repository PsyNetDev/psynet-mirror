The `rock_paper_scissors_live`, `snake_live`, `shared_canvas_live` and `public_goods_live` demos, the live-game docs and `PlayLiveGame`'s docstring and error message now recommend failing a player who is late for a live game (`late_action="fail"`), as the other multiplayer demos do. The others play on without them while the group keeps its quorum, as in `public_goods_live`'s teams of three and in snake and shared canvas groups of three or more; a player left alone is failed too (`below_quorum="fail"`). Failed players keep what they earned. `late_action="leave_group"` remains supported as an advanced option.
