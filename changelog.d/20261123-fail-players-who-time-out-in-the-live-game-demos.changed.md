The `rock_paper_scissors_live`, `snake_live`, `shared_canvas_live`, `public_goods_live` and `bargaining_live` demos, the live-game docs and `PlayLiveGame`'s docstring and error message now recommend failing a player who is late for a live game (`late_action="fail"`), as the other multiplayer demos do. The others play on without them while the group keeps its quorum, as in `public_goods_live`'s teams of three and in snake and shared canvas groups of three or more; a player left alone is failed too (`below_quorum="fail"`). `bargaining_live` also fails a player who doesn't play their game (`absent_action="fail"`), and pairs their partner again next round. Failed players keep what they earned. `late_action="leave_group"` remains supported as an advanced option. `microsociety_live` now opens its lake as soon as the first participant arrives (`initial_group_size=1`), so nobody waits for anyone, and the next participant founds a new society once the lake has closed.
