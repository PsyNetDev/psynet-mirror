Group barriers now show a pill on the progress bar when a partner is already waiting (``Your partner is ready.``, or ``{ARRIVED}/{TOTAL} of your group are ready.``). Waiting participants in groups of three or more see remaining-not-ready copy on the hold overlay (``{REMAINING} of {TOTAL} not ready yet``); pairs keep the hold title only. Longer notice copy ellipsizes instead of wrapping over the prompt. Set ``notify_arrivals=False`` to disable notices, pass ``on_arrival_message`` to customize the notice copy, or return ``hold_message(title, progress)`` from a ``waiting_message`` function to write the overlay's progress line. Pages omit the arrival-update websocket unless the participant is in an active sync group or has a removal notice to show. Notices catch up when that websocket opens after a partner is already waiting, and unreconstructable barrier specs skip notice rendering instead of returning HTTP 500.
