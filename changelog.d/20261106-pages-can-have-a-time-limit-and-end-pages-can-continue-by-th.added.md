Added `time_limit` and `time_limit_answer` to pages: the page shows a countdown at the top, where waiting messages appear, and submits `time_limit_answer` when time runs out. The server counts from when the participant reached the page, so a reload does not restart the clock, and it records `time_limit_answer` for answers that arrive too late. Timed pages cannot have a `session_id`. End logic takes `auto_continue_seconds`, so the final page continues by itself with a countdown instead of waiting for a click on Finish. The `lowest_unique_number`, `rock_paper_scissors` and `create_rate_sync` demos time their choice or vote pages and `chatroom_simple` times its chat, so a player who keeps one of those pages open without answering no longer holds up the others; a player who closes the tab is still handled by the barriers' wait limits.
