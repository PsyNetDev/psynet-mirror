Added `time_limit` and `time_limit_answer` to pages: the page shows a countdown at the top, where waiting messages appear, and submits `time_limit_answer` when time runs out. The server counts from when the participant reached the page, so a reload does not restart the clock, and it records `time_limit_answer` for answers that arrive too late. Timed pages cannot have a `session_id`. End logic takes `auto_continue_seconds`, so the final page continues by itself with a countdown instead of waiting for a click on Finish. The `lowest_unique_number`, `rock_paper_scissors`, `create_rate_sync` and `chatroom_simple` demos use time limits, so one slow player no longer holds up the others.
