Added `time_limit` and `time_limit_answer` to pages: the page counts down and submits `time_limit_answer` when time runs out. The server counts from when the participant reached the page, so a reload does not restart the clock, and it records `time_limit_answer` for answers that arrive too late. End logic takes `auto_continue_seconds`, so the final page continues by itself with a countdown instead of waiting for a click on Finish.
