A local database reset now refuses to continue when a ``flask run`` or Dallinger worker is visible but its environment cannot be read, including a server owned by another account. The shared lock file's timestamp is refreshed while a deployment holds it, so age-based ``/tmp`` cleaning cannot drop the lock out from under a long run. A second Ctrl-C during the shutdown snapshot no longer leaves a numbered archive without metadata, and a participant-finish snapshot commits the shutdown flag before the copy so a later stop can reuse that archive.
