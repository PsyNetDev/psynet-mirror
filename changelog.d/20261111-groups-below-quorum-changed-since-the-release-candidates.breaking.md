Changed what happens to groups that fall below their minimum size since the 14.0 release candidates. Groupers' `fail_participants_below_min_size` is replaced by `SimpleGrouper(below_quorum=...)`: `"fail"` fails the remaining members, and `"continue_without_group"` lets them continue without the group, and their next page tells them why ("Your partner is no longer in your group. Please continue."). Passing `fail_participants_below_min_size` raises a `TypeError`; replace `fail_participants_below_min_size=False` with `below_quorum="continue_without_group"`. `below_quorum` must be given when groups are designed to shrink: when `quorum` is below `initial_group_size`, or when a `GroupBarrier` with `late_after` acts on the group type, directly or through `also_leave`. Otherwise it defaults to `"fail"`. The experiment refuses to load when a barrier with `late_action="leave_group"` acts on a group whose grouper has `below_quorum="fail"`, which would let the late member carry on while the members who were on time are failed; a barrier built in `show_trial` raises the same error when the trial is shown, and for barriers neither check can see, such as those built in a `for_loop`, PsyNet logs a warning when that happens. `SimpleSyncGroup.dissolve_if_below_min_size()` is renamed `dissolve_if_below_quorum()`. A member leaving with removal reason `"finished"` or `"moved_on"` doesn't dissolve a group by itself, but the group's next `GroupBarrier` dissolves a group left below its quorum. While a group accepts newcomers (`join_existing_groups=True`), its barriers wait for newcomers instead, up to the grouper's `top_up_wait`; with `admit_newcomers="at_admission_point"`, only its admission points wait. "Groups below quorum" in the synchronization docs gives the full rule.
