`Regrouper` now does the bookkeeping of games played in rounds. By default it groups participants at random and sits out those who have played the most rounds. It counts each participant's rounds (`rounds_played(participant)`, `rounds_sat_out(participant)`, and `sat_out(participant)` for the latest round), and with `roles=["proposer", "responder"]` gives each group one member per role, read with `role(participant)`, and roles alternate from round to round. All of this happens under the regrouper's lock, so two rounds starting at once cannot hand out the same role twice. Sync groups record which grouper formed them and when, and each member's role is stored with their group membership, so exports show who played which role in every round.
