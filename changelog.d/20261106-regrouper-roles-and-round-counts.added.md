`Regrouper` now does the bookkeeping of games played in rounds. By default it keeps participants apart from last round's partners where possible, and sits out those who have played the most rounds. It counts each participant's rounds (`rounds_played(participant)`, `rounds_sat_out(participant)`), and with `roles=["proposer", "responder"]` gives each group one member per role, read with `role(participant)`: roles alternate by default, and `keep_roles=True` gives each participant a fixed role, choosing newcomers' roles so that as many waiting players as possible can play. All of this happens under the regrouper's lock, so two rounds starting at once cannot hand out the same role twice. Sync groups record which grouper formed them and when, and each member's role is stored with their group membership, so exports show who played which role in every round.
