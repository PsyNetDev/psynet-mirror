Added `Regrouper` for multiplayer games played in rounds. At the start of each round it splits everyone waiting into new groups, optionally within persistent teams (`within="team"`). A round starts once everyone is ready or its `deadline` passes, or at fixed ticks with `deadline=Clock(45)`. Participants who miss a round join the next one instead of holding others up or being failed. Groups can be shaped with `form_groups`, odd players out can be released to a sit-out page with `release_left_over=True`, and `on_release` runs after each split. With `max_wait_action="kick"`, a participant who waits longer than `max_wait_time`, for example with their tab hidden, continues without a group instead of being failed. A `{SECONDS}` placeholder in a barrier's `content` shows a live countdown to the next round. `GroupBarrier(skip_if_not_in_group=True)` lets participants who were kicked from their group pass that group's later barriers instead of getting an error.
