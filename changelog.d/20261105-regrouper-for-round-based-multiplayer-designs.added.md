Added `Regrouper` for multiplayer games played in rounds. At the start of each round it splits everyone waiting into new groups, optionally within persistent teams (`within="team"`). A round starts once everyone is ready or its `deadline` passes, or at fixed ticks with `deadline=Clock(45)`. Participants who miss a round join the next one instead of holding others up or being failed. Groups can be shaped with `form_groups`, odd players out can be released to a sit-out page with `release_left_over=True` (a player waiting alone first waits up to one more deadline for teammates who are still playing, so `max_wait_time` must be longer than twice the deadline), and `on_release` runs after each split. A `{SECONDS}` placeholder in a barrier's `content` shows a live countdown to the next round. To show other text while that time is unknown, for example "Waiting for another player.", pass a function of `seconds_left` as `content`; it gets `None` until the time is known. `GroupBarrier(skip_if_not_in_group=True)` lets participants who were kicked from their group pass that group's later barriers instead of getting an error; it is the default for a barrier with `timeout_between_barriers_action="kick"`.
