Added `Regrouper` for multiplayer games played in rounds. At the start of each round it splits everyone waiting into new groups, optionally within persistent cohorts (`within="cohort"`). Set `max_group_size` (and optionally `min_group_size`), or `roles`. A round starts once everyone is ready or its `deadline` passes, or at fixed wall-clock ticks with `every=45`. Participants who miss a round join the next one instead of holding others up or being failed. Groups can be shaped with `form_groups`, odd players out can be released to a sit-out page with `release_left_over=True` (a player waiting alone first waits up to one more deadline for cohort members who are still playing, so `max_wait_time` must be longer than twice the deadline or `every` period), and `on_release` runs after each split. A `{SECONDS}` placeholder in the regrouper's `waiting_message` shows a live countdown to the next round. To show other text while that time is unknown, for example "Waiting for another player.", pass a function of `seconds_left` as `waiting_message`; it gets `None` until the time is known. A participant removed from their group, for example kicked by a timeout, now passes later `GroupBarrier`s of that group type alone instead of getting an error; only a participant who was never in such a group gets one.
