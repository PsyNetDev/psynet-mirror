Added `Regrouper` for multiplayer games played in rounds. At the start of each round it splits everyone waiting into new groups, optionally within persistent cohorts (`within="cohort"`). Set `group_size` (and optionally `smallest_group`, the smallest group it forms when numbers don't divide, 2 by default), or `roles`. A round starts once everyone is ready or its `deadline` passes, or at fixed wall-clock ticks with `every=45`. Participants who miss a round join the next one instead of holding others up or being failed. Groups can be shaped with `form_groups(members, within_group, barrier)` or by overriding `Regrouper.form_groups`, as the `ultimatum_pairing` demo does to keep last round's partners apart (with roles, each group needs one member per role, and roles still alternate), odd players out can be released to a sit-out page with `left_over="sit_out"` (a player waiting alone first waits up to one more deadline for cohort members who are still playing; the regrouper's default `max_wait_time` of 300 seconds grows to twice the deadline or `every` period plus 60 seconds when that is longer, and a value you pass must be longer than twice the period), and `on_release(within_group, groups, left_over, barrier)` runs after each split, with groups that already have their `id`, so it can be saved as a pair ID. A `{SECONDS}` placeholder in the regrouper's `waiting_message` shows a live countdown to the next round. To show other text while that time is unknown, for example "Waiting for another player.", pass a function of `seconds_left` as `waiting_message`; it gets `None` until the time is known, and returns a literal `{SECONDS}` for a live countdown. Only waits with a release time (a regrouper with `deadline` or `every`, or a `SimpleGrouper` with `start_short_after`) can count down; a `{SECONDS}` placeholder in any other `waiting_message` raises an error. A participant removed from their group, for example by `late_action="leave_group"`, now passes later `GroupBarrier`s of that group type alone instead of getting an error; only a participant who was never in such a group, or whose group was closed by a `GroupCloser`, gets one.
