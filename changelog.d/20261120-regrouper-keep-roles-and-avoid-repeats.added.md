Added two `Regrouper` options for games played in rounds. With `keep_roles=True` (and `roles=`), each participant keeps the role of their first group, for example a proposer who stays a proposer: a newcomer gets the role fewest of the waiting players have, then the one fewest current members have, and players of a role with too few partners sit out, those who have played most first. With `avoid_repeats=True`, the default grouping keeps players apart from their last group's members when another grouping is possible, without changing who sits out or breaking the roles. `regrouper.role(participant)` returns a participant's role from their latest group. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo now uses `avoid_repeats=True` instead of retrying `form_groups` itself.
