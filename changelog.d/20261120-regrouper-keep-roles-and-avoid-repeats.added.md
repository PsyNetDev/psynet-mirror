Added two `Regrouper` options for games played in rounds. With `keep_roles=True` (and `roles=`), each participant keeps the role of their first group, for example a proposer who stays a proposer: a newcomer gets the role fewest of the waiting players have, then the one fewest current members have, and players of a role with too few partners are left over, those who have played most first (so a player of a scarce role still plays even if players of another role with fewer rounds sit out), and follow `left_over` (use `"sit_out"` if a role can run out). With `avoid_repeats=True`, the default grouping keeps players apart from their last group's members where it can, by starting from random groupings and swapping members between groups, or with players who would sit out: a player with more rounds still sits out before one with fewer (with `keep_roles`, one of the same role), but among players with equal rounds, who sits out may be chosen to avoid a repeat. In simulations of pairs, trios and groups with roles, including pools where some players sit out, it found a grouping without repeats whenever one existed, though as a heuristic it isn't guaranteed to. `regrouper.role(participant)` returns a participant's role from their latest group. The `ultimatum_pairing` demo now uses `avoid_repeats=True` instead of retrying `form_groups` itself.
