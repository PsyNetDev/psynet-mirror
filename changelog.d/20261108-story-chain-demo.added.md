Added the `story_chain` demo, in which groups of three take turns writing a story. Each turn is a cued round whose definition stores the writer, chosen by seat with `group_seats`; the writer stores their sentence on the round's node as soon as they submit it, and a turn that ends without one stores `None`, so every finished turn has a stored sentence and the export marks which turns finished. It shows what happens when a writer runs out of time, a member leaves, the group shrinks to one, or no group forms. The round-based tutorial and the group design page describe it, and the synchronization guide now points to `group_seats` for a stable member order.
