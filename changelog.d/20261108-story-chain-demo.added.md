Added the `story_chain` demo, in which groups of three take turns writing a story. Each turn is a cued round whose definition stores the writer, chosen by seat with `group_seats`, and whose `GroupBarrier` saves the sentence on the round's node; the export has one row per turn. It shows what happens when a writer runs out of time, a member leaves, the group shrinks to one, or no group forms. The round-based tutorial and the group design page describe it, and the synchronization guide now points to `group_seats` for a stable member order.
