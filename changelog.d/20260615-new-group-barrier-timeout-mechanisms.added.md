Added synchronous group timeout controls to `GroupBarrier` and synchronized trial makers, allowing experiments to kick or fail group members who wait too long at a barrier or fall behind after their group passed a previous barrier. The between-barrier timeout counts from the group's last barrier pass, or from when the member joined the group if that is later, so it also applies at a group's first barrier. With `timeout_between_barriers_time` set, `GroupBarrier`'s `max_wait_time` and trial makers' `sync_group_max_wait_time` default to no limit, because the timeout already moves slow members on. A value passed explicitly that isn't longer than the timeout raises an error (unless it is a function), so members who arrive promptly are not timed out while they wait for slower ones; if the slower ones may be on pages with a `time_limit`, an explicit value should also cover those pages. If a member has started a page with a `time_limit`, other than a waiting page, since the group last passed a barrier (or since joining the group), the timeout counts from when that time limit runs out, and is at least 10 seconds, so it only has to cover the time without a timed page, such as a member who closed their tab before the timed page loaded.
