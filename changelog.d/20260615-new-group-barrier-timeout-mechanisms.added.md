Added synchronous group timeout controls to `GroupBarrier` and synchronized trial makers, allowing experiments to kick or fail group members who wait too long at a barrier or fall behind after their group passed a previous barrier. The between-barrier timeout counts from the group's last barrier pass, or from when the member joined the group if that is later, so it also applies at a group's first barrier. With `timeout_between_barriers_time` set, `GroupBarrier`'s `max_wait_time` and trial makers' `sync_group_max_wait_time` default to no limit, because the timeout already moves slow members on. A value passed explicitly that isn't longer than the timeout raises an error (unless `GroupBarrier`'s `max_wait_time` is a function), so members who arrive promptly are not timed out while they wait for slower ones; if the slower ones may be on pages with a `time_limit`, an explicit value should also cover those pages. A member who has started a page with a `time_limit`, other than a waiting page, since the group last passed a barrier (or since joining the group) is not timed out until that time limit has run out plus 10 seconds, so that an answer sent at the deadline can arrive and the timeout need not be longer than the pages' time limits.
