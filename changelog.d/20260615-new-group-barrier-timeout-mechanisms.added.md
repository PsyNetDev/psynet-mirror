Added synchronous group timeout controls to `GroupBarrier` and synchronized trial makers, allowing experiments to fail group members, or remove them from the group (`"leave_group"`), when they wait too long at a barrier (`max_wait_action`) or fall behind after their group passed a previous barrier (`late_after` and `late_action`, or `sync_group_late_after` and `sync_group_late_action` on trial makers). `late_action` has no default: `late_after` raises a `ValueError` unless it is given as `"fail"` or `"leave_group"`. The between-barrier timeout counts from the group's last barrier pass, or from when the member joined the group if that is later, so it also applies at a group's first barrier. With `late_after` set, `GroupBarrier`'s `max_wait_time` and trial makers' `sync_group_max_wait_time` default to no limit, because the timeout already moves slow members on. Passing a limit together with `late_after` raises an error, so that members who arrive on time are not timed out while they wait for slower ones. A member who has started a page with a `time_limit`, other than a waiting page, since the group last passed a barrier (or since joining the group) is not timed out until that time limit has run out plus 10 seconds, so that an answer sent at the deadline can arrive and the timeout need not be longer than the pages' time limits.
