Added synchronous group timeout controls to `GroupBarrier` and synchronized trial makers, allowing experiments to kick or fail group members who wait too long at a barrier or fall behind after their group passed a previous barrier. The between-barrier timeout counts from the group's last barrier pass, or from when the member joined the group if that is later, so it also applies at a group's first barrier. With `timeout_between_barriers_time` set, `GroupBarrier`'s `max_wait_time` defaults to 20 seconds plus that timeout, and a numeric `max_wait_time` (or trial-maker `sync_group_max_wait_time`) that isn't longer than the timeout raises an error, so members who arrive promptly are not timed out while they wait for slower ones.
