Fixed a synchronized trial maker that is repeated in a loop letting members start a new run before their leader, copying the participant group and block position of the leader's previous run, including when the leader was still on the last page of that run. At the start of a later run, members wait until a member of their group, normally the leader, has started the run with the same `run_index`, and copy only from such a run; if no member starts it, as when the leader's loop ended earlier, the wait ends at `sync_group_max_wait_time`. On their first run, newcomers, including one promoted to leader, copy the group's choices from any member already initialized instead of waiting for the leader. Members whom the group's release lets through together copy one another's choices, normally the leader's, whatever their `run_index`, as in groups that re-form inside the loop.
