Added `SimpleGrouper(top_up_wait=...)`, which limits how long a group that accepts newcomers waits for one after falling below its quorum. The wait counts from when all its remaining members are at a barrier; then that barrier stops top-ups and dissolves the group, so the members left are no longer stuck waiting if recruitment has stopped. The barrier's `max_wait_time` counts from each member's own arrival, so give it `late_after`, or a `max_wait_time` longer than `top_up_wait` plus the time members may take to reach it.
