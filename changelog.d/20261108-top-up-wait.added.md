Added `SimpleGrouper(top_up_wait=...)`, which limits how long a group that accepts newcomers waits for one after falling below its quorum. The wait counts from when all its remaining members are at a barrier; then that barrier stops top-ups and dissolves the group, so the members left are no longer stuck waiting if recruitment has stopped. It only helps members still waiting, so give that barrier a `max_wait_time` longer than `top_up_wait`, or `late_after`.
