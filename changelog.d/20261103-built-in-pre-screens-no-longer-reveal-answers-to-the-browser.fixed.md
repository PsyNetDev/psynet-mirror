Fixed built-in pre-screens that let participants read the correct answer from the page. The stimuli of `LanguageVocabularyTest`, `LexTaleTest`, `ColorBlindnessTest` and the headphone tests are now downloaded by the experiment server from an http(s) `media_url` and served under opaque URLs, and `VocabTest` (including `WikiVocab` and `BibleVocab`) uses random item IDs and gives no credit unless the answer covers exactly the trial's items.
