Added `GroupBarrier(also_on_dissolve=True)`, which also runs `on_release`, with `dissolved=True`, when the group dissolves below its quorum before the barrier releases it, so that a game can still score the round its members played: once for the members waiting at the barrier and, with `below_quorum="continue_without_group"`, once for each other member when they reach the next barrier with `also_on_dissolve` of that group type in the same run of the module.
