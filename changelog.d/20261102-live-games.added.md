Added live games (`psynet.live_game`), games that a group of participants play together in real time on Dallinger's real-time engine. A game is a `LiveGame` subclass, and each kind of message its pages send is a `LiveMessage`, a Pydantic model that PsyNet checks before its `apply` changes the game's state or turns it down with `Reject`. A `PlayLiveGame` step in the timeline waits for a group's members at a `GroupBarrier`, starts a game for them, and shows them a `LiveGamePage`, whose JavaScript plays the game through `psynet.liveGame` (views with `onChange`, prediction, interpolation, status lines, countdowns and notices when a player is away). The page's answer is the game's outcome, or each player's part of it, and a game pays rewards with `performance_reward` and `add_performance_reward`. A game can be played as a cued trial by returning a `PlayLiveGame` from `show_trial`, with the trial class naming the game in `live_game_class` so that the experiment checks them together when it loads (for example, that the game and the trial's `compute_performance_reward` don't both pay), can take players who join while it runs (`join_running_game`) and let players finish early (`is_done`), and records moments along the way with `record`. With `absent_action="leave_group"`, a `PlayLiveGame` removes a player who never opens the game, or leaves it for good, from its group and the groups in `also_leave` once the game's result is saved, so later barriers don't wait for them; `sync_group_removal_reason` then returns `"no_show"` or `"left_game"`. A `LiveGameRecord`, read from `participant.live_games`, `live_game_history` or `Trial.live_game`, gives a game's status, outcome, answers, messages, moments and checkpoints. A game that fails, because its code raised an error or its record couldn't be saved, fails its players like any other error. Bots play through the game's `bot` method, and `LocalGame` in `psynet.live_game.testing` plays a game in Python for tests. The live game tutorial walks through three demo games, and the live games reference covers the rest.
