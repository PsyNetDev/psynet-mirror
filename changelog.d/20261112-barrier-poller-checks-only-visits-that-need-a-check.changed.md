The barrier poller now reads all waiting barrier visits in one batch and runs the full check only for those where something can happen, so with many waiting groups, time-based releases such as `late_after`, `top_up_wait`, `start_short_after` and `Regrouper` deadlines come within a sweep instead of after a pass over every visit. `Barrier.would_release` is renamed `Barrier.needs_check` and now also counts changes other than releases, such as removing a late member; custom barriers that overrode `would_release` (new in the 14.0 pre-releases) should rename the method.
