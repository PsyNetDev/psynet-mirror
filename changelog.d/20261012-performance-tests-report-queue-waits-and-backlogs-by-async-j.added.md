`psynet performance-test` reports async queue waits separately for high- and low-priority jobs, overall and for the start-up and steady state, with the jobs of each priority still queued at the end. The async job table adds each job's priority and mean CPU time (`priority` and `cpu_avg` in `async_job_stats`), and the summary warns when low-priority jobs that were waiting when the steady state began were still waiting at its end, a sign that the workers can't keep up with them in the long run.
