`psynet performance-test` no longer counts bots whose participants the experiment failed, for example at a prescreen or a timed-out `wait_while`, as completed successfully. It reports them as "Failed by the experiment" with a breakdown by failure reason (`participants_failed` and `failed_reasons` in the JSON results), and a test in which more than 1% of participants were failed for waiting too long (timeline holds, barriers or `while_loop` time limits) now exceeds the capacity limits. Outcome and wait-page labels in the report now say plainly what they count.
