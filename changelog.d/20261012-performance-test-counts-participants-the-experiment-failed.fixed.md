`psynet performance-test` no longer counts bots whose participants the experiment failed, for example at a prescreen or a timed-out `wait_while`, as completed successfully. The live status line and the report show them as "Failed by the experiment", the report with a breakdown by failure reason (`participants_failed` and `failed_reasons` in the JSON results), and their runtimes and wait-page times are reported too. Participants failed for waiting too long (timeline holds, barriers or `while_loop` time limits) now count as failures: a test in which more than 1% of bots timed out exceeds the capacity limits at any bot count, and the summary advises designing experiments to avoid such timeouts, for example with a longer `max_wait_time` or enough participants to fill groups and chains. Outcome and wait-page labels in the report now say plainly what they count.
