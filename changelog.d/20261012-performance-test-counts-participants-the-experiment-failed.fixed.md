`psynet performance-test` no longer counts bots whose participants the experiment failed, for example at a prescreen or a timed-out `wait_while`, as completed successfully. The live status line and the report show them as "Failed by the experiment", the report with a breakdown by failure reason (`participants_failed` and `failed_reasons` in the JSON results), and their runtimes and wait-page times are reported too. A test now exceeds the capacity limits when the share of participants failed for waiting too long (timeline holds, barriers or `while_loop` time limits) rises more than 1 percentage point above the share in the test with the fewest bots; if that smallest test already exceeds 1%, the summary points to the experiment's design instead. Outcome and wait-page labels in the report now say plainly what they count.
