Pages shown from a barrier's `waiting_logic` now end when the barrier releases the participant: the page moves on `release_grace` seconds (default 3) after the browser notices the release, which is usually within about 2 seconds, and saves an empty response with `interrupted` set in its metadata; the page's `on_complete` doesn't run, and the rest of that pass of `waiting_logic` is skipped. The participant whose arrival completes the group skips the waiting pages entirely. This includes trial pages, such as filler trials from a trial maker: a trial whose page ends or is skipped before it is answered is failed with `failed_reason="interrupted_by_release"`, so it isn't finalized and doesn't count towards the trial maker's targets of completed trials (it still counts towards `max_trials_per_block`). A barrier with `waiting_logic` can't be placed inside a trial's own pages (it raises an error there); use `waiting_screen` instead. Before, participants finished the current waiting page first. Pass `release_grace=None` to `Barrier`, `GroupBarrier` or a grouper to keep the old behavior. `release_grace` only applies with `waiting_logic`, and passing it without raises an error. During the grace period the page shows a countdown such as "Your group is ready. Moving on in 3 s."; pages can listen for the new `psynetWaitingRelease` browser event and call `preventDefault()` on it to show their own notice instead. Experiments that override `Experiment.process_response` should accept `**kwargs`, because interrupted pages pass `interrupted=True`.
