Pages shown from a barrier's `waiting_logic` now end when the barrier releases the participant: the page moves on `release_grace` seconds (default 3) after the browser notices the release, which is usually within about 2 seconds, and saves an empty response with `interrupted` set in its metadata; the page's `on_complete` doesn't run, and the rest of that pass of `waiting_logic` is skipped. The participant whose arrival completes the group skips the waiting pages entirely. Before, participants finished the current waiting page first. Pass `release_grace=None` to `Barrier`, `GroupBarrier` or a grouper to keep the old behavior. Pages can listen for the new `psynetWaitingRelease` browser event, for example to tell the participant they are about to move on. Experiments that override `Experiment.process_response` should accept `**kwargs`, because interrupted pages pass `interrupted=True`.
