Pages shown from a barrier's `waiting_logic` now end when the barrier releases the participant: the page moves on after `release_grace` seconds (default 3) and saves an empty response with `interrupted` set in its metadata. Before, participants finished the current waiting page first. Pass `release_grace=None` to `Barrier`, `GroupBarrier` or a grouper to keep the old behavior. Pages can listen for the new `psynetWaitingRelease` browser event, for example to tell the participant they are about to move on. Waiting pages also check in every 2 seconds, so `active_within` sees participants on them.
