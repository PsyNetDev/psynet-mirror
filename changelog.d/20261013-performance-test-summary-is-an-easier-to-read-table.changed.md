The `psynet performance-test` summary is now an easier-to-read table with one row per test, sorted by bots at once with the validation test last: bots at once, bots started, length, a pass/FAIL result, response times in milliseconds, failed requests and bots as counts with percentages, and bots finished, with `-` for an empty cell. A measure is red only if it broke its limit. The *largest passing test* and the *validation test* (the long test that `--n-bots auto` runs at the recommended number of participants) are labelled. Busy responses, participants the experiment failed, busy chains and queue waits (in seconds, e.g. `4.2 s`) appear only when a test had them, in a second table under the first, so that the main table fits a common terminal; the limits are listed once. A short verdict follows: the recommended number of participants at once, the largest passing test, why the next count failed (each measure with its value and limit, e.g. `At 560, 10 of 570 bots (1.8%) failed, over the 1% limit.`) and the validation test, with advice under *Notes*. The summary, worker advice and audit page call the confirmation test the validation test. In a terminal the summary, the suite header and the notes on a failed database reset are coloured and their text wraps to the terminal width, while the tables keep their full width; otherwise, as in log files or with `NO_COLOR`, they are plain text.
