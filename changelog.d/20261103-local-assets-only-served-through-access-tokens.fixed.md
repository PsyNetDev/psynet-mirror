`LocalStorage` no longer links `static/assets` to the asset storage folder, and removes such a link left by an earlier version when the experiment launches. The link let anyone who knew an asset's SHA-256 digest (for example of a published stimulus file) download it from `/static/assets/objects/sha256/<digest>` without its access token; assets are now served only through `/asset/<access_token>`. Locally stored assets are also served with the content type of their extension (for example `image/png` for a `.png` asset) rather than `application/octet-stream`.
