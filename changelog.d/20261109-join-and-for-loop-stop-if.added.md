Added `stop_if=` to `join` and `for_loop`, a function of `participant` and/or `experiment`. `join` checks it before each argument and `for_loop` before each iteration; once it returns `True`, the participant skips the rest. A started argument or iteration always runs to its end, so a page, barrier or cued trial is never cut short. For example, `join(decision_page, barrier, outcome_page, stop_if=left_pair)` skips the rest of a round once a participant has left their pair, and a `conditional` afterwards can show them what happens next.
