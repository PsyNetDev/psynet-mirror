Changed `psynet destroy ssh` to refuse to destroy an app whose pending async jobs can't be checked, for example because its web container is down, it runs an older PsyNet version, or it doesn't answer within 30 seconds, unless `--discard-pending-jobs` is passed. The message says why the check failed and how to proceed. Apps deployed with `psynet debug ssh` or `psynet performance-test ssh` are still destroyed, with a warning, even if they recruit real participants; these commands now mark the app with the new `debug_ssh_app` config variable, so apps deployed by older PsyNet versions, and sandbox-mode apps deployed by other means, are refused. `psynet deploy ssh` refuses to deploy with `debug_ssh_app` set. When destroying several apps, the others are still destroyed. `psynet export ssh` still completes with a warning that the export may lack the results of unfinished jobs.
