`GroupBarrier` `on_release` and `on_dissolve` callbacks now get as `node` the round the barrier is in or after, as stored by the members' cues, rather than a node worked out from their current trials, and `trials` holds `None` for a member whose trial is not on it. A barrier at the start of a module, before the group's first cue there, gets `node=None` and no longer marks the previous module's trials as `"stayed"`.
