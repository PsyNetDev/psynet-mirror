A member kicked by a `GroupBarrier` with `late_action="kick"` now sees "Your partner went on without you. Please continue." or "Your group went on without you. Please continue." on the progress bar until they leave their page, unless the kick dissolved the group. This is independent of `notify_arrivals`. Customize it with `on_arrival_message` (`kind="removed"`), or return `None` there to hide it. Passing `on_arrival_message` together with `notify_arrivals=False` no longer turns arrival notices on, as it did in the 14.0 release candidates; drop `notify_arrivals=False` to keep them.
