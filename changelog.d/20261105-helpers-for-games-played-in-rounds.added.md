Added helpers for games played in rounds: `pairs_avoiding_repeats` pairs players while avoiding last round's partners; `GroupPrompt` finds the participant's group when `group` is omitted and shows only the text when the participant is no longer in a group; `GroupPrompt` keeps members who dropped out of the group faded in their seat, labelled "(out of time)", "(left)" or "(dropped out)", and no longer shows members who moved on to their next group; `SyncGroup.remove_participant` takes an optional `reason` for these labels; and `advance_past_wait_pages` now keeps polling until a `timeout` (10 s by default; pass more for tests whose rounds start on a clock or deadline) and names the bots still waiting when it gives up.
