Added helpers for games played in rounds: `GroupBarrier(drop_late_members_after=...)` removes members who are late for a barrier and lets them pass the round's later barriers; `pairs_avoiding_repeats` pairs players while avoiding last round's partners; `GroupPrompt` finds the participant's group when `group` is omitted and shows only the text when the participant is no longer in a group; and `advance_past_wait_pages` accepts a `timeout` for tests whose rounds start on a clock or deadline.
