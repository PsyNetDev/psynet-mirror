Added helpers for games played in rounds. `GroupPrompt` finds the participant's group of type `group_type` when `group` is omitted (`group_type` may also be left out while the participant has only ever been in groups of one type), and shows only the text when the participant is no longer in a group or `group=None`. `GroupPrompt` also keeps members who dropped out of the group faded in their seat, labelled "(removed)" if a barrier removed them for being late, "(left)" or "(dropped out)", and no longer shows members who moved on to their next group, except with their answers on a results page, where a member whose answer is `None` keeps their seat without a speech bubble. `SyncGroup.remove_participant` takes an optional `reason` (`"moved_on"` or `"late"`) for these labels. `advance_past_wait_pages` now keeps polling until a `timeout` (10 s by default; pass more for tests whose rounds start on a clock or deadline) and names the bots still waiting when it gives up.
