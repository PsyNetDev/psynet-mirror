Added helpers for games played in rounds: `pairs_avoiding_repeats` pairs players while avoiding last round's partners; `GroupPrompt` finds the participant's group when `group` is omitted and shows only the text when the participant is no longer in a group; `GroupPrompt` draws members who left the group, for example because a barrier removed them, faded in their seat; and `advance_past_wait_pages` now keeps polling until a `timeout` (10 s by default; pass more for tests whose rounds start on a clock or deadline). Its `max_iterations` argument is deprecated.
