Added protection for pending async jobs on docker-ssh deployments: idle hibernation keeps the app awake while jobs are pending, `psynet export ssh` and `psynet export local` end with a warning that the export misses their results, and `psynet destroy ssh` wakes the app, reports its pending jobs, and asks for confirmation (or refuses when it cannot ask) unless `--discard-pending-jobs` is passed. Jobs that never started within an hour of being enqueued, or ran past their timeout, are reported as probably lost and do not block hibernation or destroying.
