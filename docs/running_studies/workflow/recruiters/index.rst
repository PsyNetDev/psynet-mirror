Choosing a recruiter
====================

A recruiter is the service that invites participants to your experiment
and pays them. This guide covers three recruiters; choose one before you
finalize your experiment configuration, because each has its own
payment, consent, and qualification requirements.

- `Prolific <https://www.prolific.com/>`__ offers a high-quality,
  diverse participant pool that suits most academic studies.
- `CINT <https://www.cint.com/>`__ (formerly Lucid) provides a larger
  pool across many countries and languages, which makes it useful for
  cross-cultural studies.
- **Lab Recruiter** lets a lab recruit from its own participant pool
  without a third-party marketplace. You need access to a Lab Recruiter
  instance run by your institution.

For configuration keys such as ``recruiter``, ``wage_per_hour``,
``base_payment``, ``initial_recruitment_size``, and
``soft_max_experiment_payment``, see the
:doc:`configuration reference </reference/configuration>`.
Shared pre-launch checks (duration, storage, title and description)
are on :doc:`../setting_up_the_experiments`.

If this is your first Prolific study on your own account, start with
the :doc:`Getting started walkthrough </getting_started/collecting_data>`.
Use the Prolific page below when you are running a study in a lab
workflow (shared workspace, qualification files, and dashboard
procedures).
