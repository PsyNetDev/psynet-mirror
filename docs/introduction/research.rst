Research using PsyNet
=====================

These are example papers that used PsyNet, grouped by the kinds of experiment
described in :ref:`applications`. A paper that combines several techniques
appears under each of them.

Citing PsyNet
-------------

Until a dedicated PsyNet paper is published, please cite:

   Harrison, P. M. C., Marjieh, R., Adolfi, F., van Rijn, P.,
   Anglada-Tort, M., Tchernichovski, O., Larrouy-Maestri, P., & Jacoby, N.
   (2020). Gibbs sampling with people. *Advances in Neural Information
   Processing Systems*, *33*, 10659–10671.
